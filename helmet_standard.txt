BIS Standards for Helmets in India

1. BIS Standards and Safety of Helmets
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) has established safety requirements for protective helmets used by motorcycle and scooter riders in India. The main Indian Standard for protective helmets for two-wheeler riders is IS 4151:2015 – Protective Helmets for Two Wheeler Riders. The purpose of this standard is to ensure that helmets provide adequate protection to the head during accidents and reduce the risk of serious head injuries.

2. Construction and Material Requirements
A helmet must be designed and manufactured using suitable materials that can withstand impact and provide effective protection. BIS requirements cover aspects such as the helmet shell, protective padding, retention system, visor or other components, dimensions, and workmanship. The helmet should fit properly and remain securely positioned on the rider's head during normal use and in an accident. Materials used in the helmet should also be suitable for prolonged contact with the user's head and should not create unnecessary safety hazards.

3. Performance and Safety Testing
Helmets are subjected to various tests to verify their protective performance. These tests can include impact absorption, penetration resistance, rigidity, retention-system performance, and field-of-vision requirements. The helmet must demonstrate that it can absorb and manage impact energy effectively and that the retention system can keep the helmet securely on the rider's head. Testing helps ensure that helmets provide consistent protection under specified conditions.

4. BIS Certification and Standard Mark
Motorcycle helmets covered by the applicable mandatory certification requirements must comply with the relevant Indian Standard and undergo the prescribed BIS conformity-assessment process. A compliant helmet carries the BIS Standard Mark, along with the applicable licence or registration details as required. Consumers should check the marking and purchase helmets from reliable manufacturers or sellers rather than relying only on appearance or price.

5. Importance of Wearing a BIS-Compliant Helmet
A properly designed and correctly fitted helmet is an important part of motorcycle and scooter safety. BIS requirements help establish minimum levels of protection so that helmets available in the Indian market meet specified safety criteria. Riders should select the correct helmet size, ensure that the retention strap is properly fastened, and replace a helmet after a significant impact or when it has deteriorated. BIS certification provides an important indication that the helmet has been assessed against the applicable Indian safety requirements.
