BIS Standards for Packaged Drinking Water in India

1. BIS Standard and Quality of Packaged Drinking Water
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) has specified requirements for packaged drinking water to ensure that it is safe and suitable for human consumption. The main standard is IS 14543:2016 – Packaged Drinking Water (Other Than Packaged Natural Mineral Water). The standard specifies requirements relating to the quality, treatment, packaging, and testing of drinking water sold in packaged form. It helps protect consumers from physical, chemical, and microbiological contamination.

2. Source and Treatment of Water
Packaged drinking water must be obtained from an appropriate source and undergo suitable treatment before packaging. Depending on the quality of the source water, treatment processes may include filtration, disinfection, reverse osmosis, or other appropriate methods. The treatment should ensure that the finished water meets the specified requirements. The water must be protected from contamination during processing, storage, and filling.

3. Chemical and Microbiological Requirements
One of the most important aspects of the BIS standard is the testing of water for microbiological and chemical parameters. The water must comply with specified limits for various substances and must meet microbiological safety requirements. Tests are carried out to detect contamination that could make the water unsafe for consumption. Regular quality control and laboratory testing are therefore essential for packaged drinking-water manufacturers.

4. Packaging and Hygiene Requirements
The containers used for packaged drinking water must be suitable for contact with drinking water and should protect the product from contamination. Bottles, jars, pouches, and other permitted packaging materials must meet the applicable requirements. The filling and sealing operations should be carried out under hygienic conditions, and the containers should be properly cleaned or suitably prepared before filling. Proper storage and transportation are also important for maintaining water quality.

5. BIS Certification and Marking
Packaged drinking water is subject to mandatory BIS certification in India. Manufacturers are required to comply with the applicable BIS requirements and obtain the necessary certification before marketing covered packaged drinking water. Certified products carry the BIS Standard Mark and the relevant licence information as prescribed. Consumers should check the BIS marking and product details on the package when purchasing packaged drinking water, as this provides an indication that the product is covered by the applicable BIS certification requirements.
